Little known, important POIS

In the furthest northeast portion of the map, you can find the Akkala Ancient Tech Lab. At said tech lab, once you restore the blue flame to the furnace, (blue flame starting point on nearby mountian) you can purchase ancient gear in exchange for rupees and guardian parts.

All around the map, you can find large cacti-resembiling fruits. if you approach these and offer them enough ruppees, then they will sprout into Great Fairy Fountian. at these fountians, you can use materials to upgrade armour to increase its defensive power and with some sets, even get set bonuses with twice-upgraed armor sets equiped.

From the highland stable, if you travel over the Horse God Bridge, you will find what apears to be a great fairy, but turns out to be the Horse God. With the horse god, you can ressurect fallen horses for the price of one Endura Carrot. However, the first resurection is free as a reward for restoring the horse god's power.

Pro Tips

  • Forage often. Finding good things to cook and brew with is important, especialy with all the high-damage dealing enemys late-game. so make sure you frequently visit forests across the map and other locations to get ingredients.
  • Get the master sword, and divine beasts. I hear you, speedrunners it seems like a waste of time but with divine beasts like Vah Medoh or half completion of Vah Ruta for Ravali's gale and Zora Armor, inflitrating Hyrule Castle gets 20 times easier.
  • Dont sleep on stamina vesels. Stamina Vesels can be very powerful late game, especcialy when paired with items like sledgehammers or Boulder Breakers which spin around in a charged attack. Therefore, having more stamina allows you to increase your damage per second.
