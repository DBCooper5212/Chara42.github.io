Leviathans:

In the east and northwest, you will often find the Reaper Leviathan. They hunt with sound, so if you keep the noise down you should be fine, but keep creature decoys handy just in case.

Meanwhile, in the lost river, void (outside the map) and deeper parts of the main map, you may find Ghost Leviathan. They can see, but will only ram you to attack, however are very agile, so distracting them is the best play. (note: Ghost leviathans in the void will respawn, so killing them just delays the inevitable.)

However, in the Inactive and Active lava zones, you will find the Sea Dragon Leviathan They can, and will see you, and when they see you, will swipe at you with their front fins and shoot fireballs at you. distracting them is key, however te're kind of like pupies once you understand how to escape them.

Pro tips:

  • For the aroura raid, bring a propulsion canon, fire extingusher, repair tool, and a laser cutter in orer to fully explore the ship.
  • when you go down to the Sea Emperor's Aquarium, bring two blue tablets. you can get the first, and the blueprint for the second, in the alien thermal plant in the ILZ cave system.
  • Also for the Sea Emperors aquarium, bring in a prawn suit. if you don't, you'll be defensless to the potential Reaper Leviathan waiting outside the second end of the phase gate.
  • Build a Cyclops as early as possible. a mobile base is extremley good to have when you travel around the map, and on top of needing its internal fabricator to make the sheild upgrade needed to beat the game, you can dock a prawn suit in it, so a Cyclops is a must
  • Finaly, always collect more than you think you'll need. In the endgame, you need more of everything, so extra is a good thing.
